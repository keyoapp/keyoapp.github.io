# Keyo 🎹

**Learning piano shouldn't feel like homework.**

Keyo is a piano app for iPhone and iPad with tiny lessons, silly streaks and real songs. Read the notes, train your ears, press the keys, and meet a mascot who gets *very* sleepy if you ghost him.

No sign-up wall. Open it, press a key, make a sound.

## What Keyo does

- 🎼 **Read notes.** Find middle C, name the keys and read the staff.
- 👂 **Train your ears.** Hear a note and tap it, or say which of two is higher.
- ⚫ **Black keys.** Sharps, flats, and why C♯ and D♭ are the same key.
- 🥁 **Keep the beat.** Listen to a rhythm, then tap it back.
- 🙌 **Two hands.** Play two keys at once on a grand staff.
- 🎶 **Real songs.** Play along while the notes scroll past, with backing chords under you.
- 🎹 **Free play.** A piano that's always ready, with a metronome and a recorder.

Streaks, hearts, XP, badges and weekly leagues keep you coming back. Keyo is built for everyone: Dynamic Type at every size, VoiceOver on every key, Reduce Motion, and dark mode.

## Keyo Platinum

The first units, streaks and the daily habit are free. Keyo Platinum (monthly, or yearly with a free trial) opens the full course, plus practice tools, Keyo Coach, listening to your own piano through the microphone, unlimited hearts, bonus songs, more piano sounds and outfits.

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel them any time in **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**.

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 22, 2026**

This policy explains what information the Keyo app ("Keyo", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

### The short version

- You can use Keyo without an account. Without one, your progress stays on your device.
- If you sign in, we store your progress so it syncs and so you can take part in leagues.
- We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
- Microphone audio is analysed on your device and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

### Information stored only on your device

Whether or not you sign in, Keyo keeps the following on your iPhone or iPad so the app works: lesson progress, streaks, hearts, XP, badges, settings (sound, haptics, piano sound, daily goal, reminder times) and recordings you make in Free play. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

### Information we collect if you sign in

Signing in is optional and uses **Sign in with Apple**. When you sign in, we receive and store:

- **Account details:** a unique account ID and the email address Apple shares with us. You can choose "Hide My Email", in which case we only see a private relay address.
- **Profile:** the username and avatar you choose, your daily goal and your time zone (used to work out when your day and streak end).
- **Learning activity:** which lessons you completed, when, how long they took, your accuracy and the XP earned.
- **League data:** your league, weekly XP and rank.
- **Subscription status:** whether you have Keyo Platinum and when it expires.
- **Safety data:** people you block, and reports you make about other players (the reason and the reported username).

### What other players can see

If you join a league, other players in it can see only your **username, avatar, level, streak, this week's XP and badges**. Your email address and the rest of your profile are never shown to other players.

### Microphone

Real piano mode (Keyo Platinum) uses the microphone to hear the notes you play on your own piano. Keyo asks for permission first, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in the iOS Settings app. Audio is processed in real time on your device to detect pitch. **It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.**

### Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, Keyo schedules them locally on your device. No notification data is sent to our servers.

### Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. We use **RevenueCat** to check which purchases and subscriptions are active; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID (linked to your Keyo account ID if you sign in) and your purchase history from Apple.

### Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Keyo. They process data only on our behalf:

| Provider | What it does | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) | Sign in with Apple, in-app purchases, App Store | Account identifier, email (or relay address), purchases |
| [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) | Accounts and database (hosting your synced progress and leagues) | The signed-in data listed above |
| [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/) | Subscription and purchase status | App user ID, purchase history |

We do not use advertising networks, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

### How we use information

Only to run Keyo: to sync your progress, keep your streak, run leagues, give you the features you paid for, keep leagues safe (handling reports and blocks), and fix problems. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

### How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, lesson history, XP, league membership, blocks and reports you made are deleted from our servers. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for their own legal and accounting obligations.

### Deleting your account and data

- **Delete your account** in the app: **Profile → Delete account** (in the Account section). This permanently removes your account and the data linked to it from our servers.
- **Sign out** to stop syncing; your local progress stays on the device.
- **Delete the app** to remove everything stored on your device.

You can also contact us (below) to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

### Children

Keyo is designed to be friendly for learners of all ages, but accounts and leagues are not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) without a parent or guardian's consent. Children can use Keyo without an account, in which case nothing leaves the device. If you believe a child has given us personal information without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

### Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database uses row-level security so each account can only read and change its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

### Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us to make a request; we'll respond within 30 days.

### Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For significant changes we'll also let you know in the app.
